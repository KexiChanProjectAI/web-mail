## Response body contract

All 404s return:
  - status: 404
  - content-type: text/plain; charset=utf-8
  - body: "Not found"

200s return:
  - mode=text → content-type: text/plain; charset=utf-8, body: cache.text
  - mode=html → content-type: text/html; charset=utf-8, body: cache.html

No 200 with empty body (intentional divergence from upstream; see
learnings.md for the rationale table).
